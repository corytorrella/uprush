Transparency

How Uprush actually makes money.

Nothing here is designed to influence which cases get resolved, escalated, or rated — that firewall is the whole point.

Uprush Verified accreditation

An annual fee, tiered by company size — modeled on how BBB accreditation works. It's a trust-mark fee earned through responsiveness metrics, never a pay-to-play mechanic.

Uprush Signal

Aggregated, anonymized industry trend reports sold to enterprises, analysts, and regulators. High-margin, and it scales independently of any single case's outcome.

Uprush Alternatives

A disclosed, opt-in referral fee when you switch to a suggested alternative provider. It only ever appears after a case is already severity-flagged and aging — never before, and never tied to escalation or rating.

Questions

Pricing, plainly answered.

Is Uprush really free for consumers, forever?

Yes. Filing, joining a case, uploading evidence, and confirming resolution are free today and will stay free. It's the foundation the entire trust model depends on — a paywall on the consumer side would undercut the reason companies take the record seriously.

Can a company pay to make cases disappear or rank lower?

No. There is no mechanism, at any tier, that removes a case or changes its escalation tier for money. Resolved and unresolved cases both stay visible permanently — that's what keeps the record credible.

How is the Uprush Verified fee different from "pay to play"?

The fee grants access to the dashboard and the right to display the seal — it does not grant the seal itself. Verified Partner status, the top tier, is earned through sustained response performance and can be lost if that performance drops.

Can I switch plans or cancel anytime?

Yes. Growth and Enterprise are billed on standard renewal terms with no long-term lock-in required to get started — Enterprise contracts include the custom terms your compliance team would expect.

What happens if my company stays unclaimed?

Cases keep filing against your Listed profile whether you claim it or not. Claiming costs nothing on the Starter tier — it just gives you the ability to respond and be judged on that response, instead of on silence.
