Terms of Service

These terms cover your use of Uprush — the network where consumers file verified cases against companies, and companies resolve them in the open. By filing a case, claiming a company profile, or otherwise using Uprush, you agree to what's below.

1. Acceptance of Terms

Creating an account, filing a case, joining one with Add Your Case, or claiming a company profile means you accept these terms and our Privacy Policy. If you're using Uprush on behalf of a company, you're confirming you have the authority to do so.

2. The Service

Uprush lets a consumer file a documented case against a company, lets other consumers with the same problem join that case, and routes verified cases into a company's existing support systems. We structure and verify cases; we don't adjudicate them, and we're not a party to the underlying dispute between you and the company.

3. Consumer Accounts & Verification

Filing a case requires a confirmed email address and, optionally, proof of relationship with the company — an order number, account reference, or receipt. You're responsible for keeping your account credentials secure and for the accuracy of what you submit.

4. Case Content & Accuracy

What you file has to be true to your actual experience. Cases found to be fabricated, duplicated in bad faith, or filed to harass rather than resolve a genuine issue can be removed, and repeated abuse can result in account suspension. Companies get a formal path to dispute anything they believe is inaccurate — see our Moderation Policy.

5. Company Accounts & Accreditation

Claiming a company profile requires verifying your authority to represent that company. Uprush Verified accreditation is granted and can be revoked based on responsiveness metrics — it is never guaranteed by payment alone, and the Verified Partner tier specifically requires sustained performance to keep.

6. Prohibited Conduct

  • Filing or backing a case you know to be false
  • Creating multiple accounts to inflate a case's backer count
  • Using Uprush to harass, threaten, or extort a company or individual
  • Attempting to circumvent our verification, rate-limiting, or fingerprinting safeguards
  • Scraping case data for purposes outside normal use of the service

7. Intellectual Property

You retain ownership of what you submit — case descriptions, evidence, ratings — but grant Uprush a license to display, structure, and route it as the service requires. The Uprush name, the Ascend Mark, and the Uprush Verified seal are our trademarks.

8. Disclaimers

Uprush is not a law firm, a government regulator, or a debt collector, and using it is not a substitute for legal advice or a regulatory complaint. We don't guarantee that any case will be resolved, or resolved within a particular time frame — the escalation ladder describes how visibility changes, not an outcome we control.

9. Limitation of Liability

To the fullest extent the law allows, Uprush isn't liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages arising from your use of the service, including the outcome of any case. Our total liability for any claim is limited to the amount you paid us in the twelve months before the claim, which for consumer accounts is zero.

10. Dispute Resolution

Most concerns about a case are handled through our own moderation and dispute process, described in full in Methodology & Trust. For legal disputes about these terms themselves, you agree to resolve them individually, not as part of a class action, through binding arbitration — except where local law doesn't allow that.

11. Changes to These Terms

We'll post updates here with a new effective date, and for material changes we'll make a reasonable effort to notify account holders directly before the change takes effect.

12. Contact

Privacy Policy

This describes what we collect, how we use it, and — because the whole platform depends on it — exactly what never becomes public or gets shown to a company.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: your email address and authentication details
  • Verification details: optional proof of relationship such as an order number, account reference, or receipt
  • Case content: what you file, including any evidence you attach — receipts, screenshots, correspondence
  • Usage data: device and account fingerprints used for rate-limiting and anti-abuse safeguards

2. How We Use It

We use this information to verify that a case represents a real person and a real relationship with the company involved, to route your case correctly, to notify you of status changes, and to enforce the rate-limiting and minimum-verification safeguards described in Methodology & Trust.

3. Public vs. Private Information

Your name, email address, and account or order number are never shown on a public case page, and never shown to the company — they see a case reference number and category only. You can choose to reveal your identity to a company for direct contact, but that's always your choice, never the default.

4. Data Sharing

We share case metadata — reference number, category, evidence, and status — with the company a case is filed against, since that's how the service works. We share case evidence with a regulator only when you use Regulatory Bridge to do so yourself. We do not sell your personal information, and Uprush Signal — our aggregated data product — is built from de-identified, aggregated case data, never individual records.

5. Retention & Deletion

Evidence you upload is retained for the life of the case, then deleted on a published schedule, unless it's part of an active dispute or a regulatory referral you initiated. You can request deletion of your account and associated personal information at any time, subject to what we need to keep for active or recently resolved cases.

6. Sensitive Categories

Financial, insurance, and health-adjacent cases often include more sensitive evidence — account numbers, medical references. These categories get additional handling matching the disclosure and retention expectations already common in those regulated industries.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information, and to object to certain uses of it. Contact us using the details below to exercise any of these rights.

8. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards — encryption in transit and at rest, access controls, and the same device/account fingerprinting used against abuse — to protect what you share with us. No system is perfectly secure, and we'll notify affected users if that ever changes the calculus for your data.

9. Contact

Privacy questions or requests can be sent to privacy@uprush.example.
