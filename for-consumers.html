Your Case, Start To Finish

From "no one's answering" to a resolution you confirmed yourself.

01

Search first

We match your issue against existing cases before you file anything. Most problems already have one — you just add your voice.

02

File or add your case

No match found means a short, structured filing — company, category, what happened, what resolution you want, optional evidence.

03

Verify, privately

Confirm your email and, optionally, proof of relationship — an order number, account reference, or receipt. None of it is ever shown publicly.

04

Follow along

Get notified at every status change on any case you've joined — Filed, Under Review, Building Support, Escalated, Resolved.

05

Confirm resolution

Only you — or a majority of active backers on a merged case — can mark it Resolved. A company can say "Response Sent." That's not the same thing.

06

A short, structured rating — was it resolved fairly, was it fast — feeds directly into the company's public Uprush Verified score.

Questions

Before you file, plainly answered.

Will the company ever see my name?

Not unless you choose to share it. The company only ever sees a case reference number, your category, and any evidence you attach — never your name, email, or account details.

What if the company just ignores my case?

Escalation doesn't require the company's cooperation. If they don't acknowledge your case within the SLA window, it moves up the ladder automatically — that's the point of a clock the company doesn't control.

Can I file against any company?

Yes. Every company has a profile on Uprush whether they've claimed it or not — filing against an unclaimed company still starts the record and the notification.

Can I withdraw a case I filed by mistake?

Yes, you can withdraw your own case at any time before it's resolved. If others have joined it with Add Your Case, the case stays open under the next active filer.

Does joining an existing case cost anything?

No. Filing, joining, following, and confirming resolution are free for consumers — permanently.
