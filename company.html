Why We Exist

Escalation infrastructure that happens to be visible.

Uprush isn't a review site, a petition platform, or a public-shaming tool. It's the layer that sits between a consumer's complaint and a company's existing support system — owned by neither side.

The Consumer Problem

One complaint, filed once, gets nowhere.

  • Normal support channels have no escalation path once they stop responding
  • Every other person with the same problem has to start from zero, too
  • There's no leverage-efficient way to get real attention

The Company Problem

Every complaint looks the same from the inside.

  • No reliable signal for which issues are real, recurring, and costly
  • No way to distinguish a systemic bug from isolated noise
  • No shared record either side can actually trust

What We Won't Compromise

Protective, not aggressive. Verified, not viral.

Protective, not aggressive

Volume decides visibility. It never decides the outcome. Every mechanic is built to answer "could this look like a pile-on?" with no.

Verified, not viral

Every backer is a confirmed claimant on a real case, not an anonymous vote. The record means something because it can't be gamed by noise.

Complementary, not a substitute

Regulatory Bridge routes legitimate cases to the CFPB, DOT, and state regulators faster — it never pretends to replace their authority.

Free for consumers, permanently

Not a growth tactic — the credibility foundation the entire trust model depends on.

Where We Sit

Verified complaint infrastructure.

Adjacent to a few categories you already know — but owned by neither, and built to be the layer between all three.

Trust & Accreditation

Like BBB accreditation — a trust mark companies earn through responsiveness, displayed publicly and backed by real performance data.

Public Reputation

Like Trustpilot's visibility — except every case is a verified, structured record instead of an unstructured review, and it stays two-sided by design.

Case Routing

Like Zendesk or Salesforce's case management — except the record is owned by the platform between consumer and company, not by either one alone.

Where We're Headed

Built in three phases, on purpose.

Phase 1 — Foundation

Prove the mechanic

Core consumer filing, search and dedupe, and case pages. Basic business claim-and-respond flow with manual moderation — proving companies actually respond to claimed profiles.

Phase 2 — Infrastructure

Automate the ladder

The automated escalation ladder, the Uprush Verified accreditation launch, native Zendesk/Salesforce/ServiceNow integrations, the SLA dashboard, and right-of-reply tooling.

Phase 3 — Intelligence & Scale

See the whole market

Uprush Signal as a full data product, real Regulatory Bridge intake integrations, the Uprush Alternatives referral program, and an enterprise compliance module for regulated industries.
